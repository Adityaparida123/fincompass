Banking basics:
- Savings accounts earn interest and keep money liquid.
- Fixed deposits lock money for a fixed term at a higher rate.
- Deposits are insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank by DICGC.
- RBI is the central bank regulating banks in India.

Keep bank credentials private. Banks never ask for OTPs, PINs, or CVVs.
