Before borrowing, ask:
- Do I truly need this now, or can I save for it?
- Can I afford the EMI alongside existing commitments?
- Do I have an emergency buffer so the EMI doesn't push me into a crisis?

EMI (equated monthly instalment) repays principal plus interest over the tenure. Lower tenures mean higher EMIs but less total interest.

Rules of thumb:
- Keep total debt payments well below 40% of monthly income.
- Never borrow to fund lifestyle spending.
- Compare total interest, not just monthly EMI.
