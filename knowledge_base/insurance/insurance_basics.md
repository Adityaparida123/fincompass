Insurance transfers financial risk to an insurer in exchange for a premium.

- Health insurance covers medical costs; essential for managing healthcare expenses.
- Life insurance supports dependants in case of the insured's death.
- Term insurance is the simplest, low-cost life cover.
- IRDAI regulates insurance in India.

Buy insurance to protect against risk, not as an investment with a promised return.
