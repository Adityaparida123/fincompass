Personal taxation basics:
- Income tax is levied by the central government on income.
- There are two regimes in India: old (with deductions) and new (lower rates, fewer deductions).
- Section 80C allows deductions on specified investments (up to the statutory limit).
- TDS is tax deducted at source by employers and payers.
- Filing returns on time avoids penalties; consult the Income Tax Department for current slabs.

Tax rules change each year. Verify current slabs and limits with official sources.
