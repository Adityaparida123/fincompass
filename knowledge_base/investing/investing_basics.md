Investment concepts:
- Risk and return go together; higher returns usually mean higher risk.
- Diversification spreads risk across assets.
- SIP (systematic investment plan) invests a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds.
- Equity mutual funds are market-linked; no returns are guaranteed.
- SEBI regulates securities markets and mutual funds in India.

Do not invest money you need within the next few years. No one can guarantee returns.
