A credit score is a number that reflects how responsibly you have handled credit.

- In India, scores typically range 300-900 (CIBIL) or 1-999 (others).
- Payment history, credit utilisation, and credit mix influence the score.
- Regularly reviewing your credit report helps catch errors.
- Your FinAI readiness score is educational; it is not a substitute for a bureau credit score.

Never pay to "improve" a score through dubious services.
