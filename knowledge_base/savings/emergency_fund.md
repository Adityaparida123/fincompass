An emergency fund covers essential expenses if income stops or an unexpected cost arises.

Guidance:
- Target 3 to 6 months of essential expenses.
- Keep it in a liquid, low-risk account (savings account or liquid fund).
- Rebuild it after use; treat it as a priority, not an option.

Months covered = total savings / essential monthly expenses.
