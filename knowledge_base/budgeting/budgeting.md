A budget is a plan for where your money goes. The best budget is one based on your actual spending, not a rigid rule.

A common starting point is the 50-30-20 guideline:
- 50% needs (essentials)
- 30% wants (discretionary)
- 20% savings and debt repayment

Adjust percentages to your reality. Track spending for 2-3 months, then set category limits that reflect your real patterns and leave room to save.
