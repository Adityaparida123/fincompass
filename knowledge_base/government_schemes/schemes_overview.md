Public schemes can reduce financial pressure:

- Banking: PMJDY zero-balance accounts.
- Pension: Atal Pension Yojana, NPS.
- Housing: PM Awas Yojana.
- Business: Mudra, Stand Up India.
- Savings: Sukanya Samriddhi, Senior Citizens Savings Scheme.

FinAI matches you only as "potentially eligible". Always verify eligibility and apply through official portals (india.gov.in and scheme-specific sites).
