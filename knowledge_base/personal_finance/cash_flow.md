Cash flow is the money moving in and out of your account each month.

- Income: salary, business income, rent, interest.
- Essential expenses: rent, groceries, utilities, transport, healthcare.
- Discretionary expenses: dining out, entertainment, shopping.
- Debt payments: EMIs and other obligations.

Available cash flow = income - (essential + discretionary + debt payments).

A consistently positive cash flow is the foundation of savings and financial resilience.
